# Dossier layout

Adapt depth to evidence. Use ordinary headings, exact tables, working source URLs, and date checked. Keep the chat response shorter than the saved dossier.

## Identification and best access path

State full FCC IDs, model/variant, grantee, module/host boundary, revision, and confidence. Lead with the best-supported opportunity and its limiting uncertainty.

## Applications and revisions

| FCC ID | Application ID / grant date | Purpose / class | Model or host | Evidence / differences |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Do not equate equipment classes with different physical boards without evidence.

## Complete document inventory

| Application | Exhibit / document ID | Type | Availability / date | Direct file / source index | Retrieved file | Engineering value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use one row per discoverable exhibit. Separately list important missing categories per application and supported status: not listed, confidential, metadata-only, or retrieval failed. Show official/mirror URLs when known; distinguish landing pages from direct files.

## Hardware map

| Location / marking | Component / role | Specifications | Evidence (exhibit + page/figure) | Confidence | Datasheet / source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Add an inspected board image/map when useful. Separate readings, tentative identification, and unknowns.

## Access interfaces

| Interface / location | Pinout / voltage / orientation | Evidence | Constraints | Next validation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Explicitly mark unverified pin assignments and voltage.

## Radio map

| Radio / mode | Bands / channels | Modulation / bandwidth | Power and measurement basis | Antenna path | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Retain differences between grants, report conditions, silicon capabilities, and established application protocols.

## Firmware, tools, and opportunities

| Resource / opportunity | Exact hardware compatibility | Source | Enables | Remaining constraint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Include supported public firmware, recovery, SDK/source, drivers, teardowns, local-control options. State backup, signing, encryption, and protection status as known or unknown.

## First bench session

Give three to five steps with gear, supported checks/connections/commands, expected observations, and decision/stop conditions. Identify irreversible operations at the relevant step. Prefer read-only collection and verified backups before writing.

## Unknowns and coverage

State sources/applications checked, retrieval date, inaccessible exhibits, conflicts, revision risks, and the smallest additional photo/measurement that resolves important gaps. Report actual archive coverage, not presumed database completeness.

## Artifacts and sources

Link dossier, input manifest, download results, and source archive as available. Keep titles, exhibit IDs, page references, and exact URLs in the file so it works independently. Use ordinary links rather than session citation tokens in saved files; use the environment's required citations in chat.
